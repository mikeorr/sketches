How to play

Dice is like the Spider card game but the goal is to order dice into sequences of 1-6 (the six dice faces: ⚀ ⚁ ⚂ ⚃ ⚄ ⚅ ).

The game starts with several dice dealt randomly into four rows. Additional dice are in the stock for later play.

To move, drag a die to another row. All the dice to the right of it will move too. When you assemble a complete suit of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, it will automatically be removed from the tableau and you'll get a point at the top. When you remove all suits (8 points), you win the game.

If you want to undo a move, do the opposite move.

When you run out of desired moves, press "Draw" to deal a random die to each row from the stock. You can do this six times.

The Spider card game has additional rules this version doesn't enforce. In Spider you can move only a descending sequence, and you must move it to a column whose bottom card is the next-higher rank or an empty column. For instance, you can move 4-3-2 to a column ending in 5. Rules differ on whether all these cards must be the same suit. And in Spider you can't have an empty column when pressing "Draw". Spysol doesn't enforce these restrictions.

About Dice

Dice was created by Mike Orr. This is version 1.

It's written in modern vanilla Javascript and CSS. The only library is random-seedable by Chris Akroyd to roll the dice. The dice are Unicode dice characters.
